# Critique — Vkick app UI (`src`)

Re-verified against HEAD `2e472bd` ("feat(calendar): month-view date picker sheet"). The four findings from
the previous snapshot (`2026-10-13`, score 33) are now **fixed**: the Login debug dump is gone, `bg-app` is
gone, the inert `__ADS_ENABLED__` switch and its orphaned `src/lib/ads.ts` are gone, and the ad no longer
sits under the fixed match CTA. The score moved 33 → 31 because the fixes landed alongside new surface
(the calendar sheet, the toast component, the new type/space systems) and because the typography contract
turned out to be unimplemented in a way the earlier review could not see from source alone.

## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does this look AI-generated? Mostly no — with one honest exception.**

**LLM assessment:** the identity is enforced, not decorated: a token contract (volt = action, gold = your
club, danger = destructive, ink scale = depth), a floodlit pitch canvas, drawn icons only, no gradients, no
glass, no identical card grid, real RTL with mirrored chevrons and Persian digits. The failure mode is not
slop, it is *unimplemented intent*: the type system, the entry stagger and the font families are specified
and never shipped.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json src index.html`, exit **2**, 4 findings:
- `side-tab` ×3 — `src/components/Toast.tsx:23,29,34`, `border-s-4` (4px accent bar on the leading edge of
  every toast). Real, and against the design system's own rule: `reference/colorize.md:84` — "Hairline
  borders … **not side-stripes; see the absolute ban on `border-left/right > 1px`**". This is the shape the
  detector calls the single most recognizable AI tell.
- `bounce-easing` ×1 — `src/index.css:53`, `--ease-pop: cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`. Partly
  intentional ("earned spectacle"), but over-applied: 7 animation sites use it (`.pin-drop`, `.marker-pop`,
  `.star-pop`, `.toast-in`, `.rating-pulse`, `.intro-glyph`, `.sheet-in`).

**Browser overlay:** injection succeeded on `/matches`, `/match/:id`, `/profile`, `/table` (Chrome, 390×720).
Findings per screen: matches 10 (7× `ai-color-palette`, `cramped-padding`, `bounce-easing`,
`layout-transition`), match 4, profile 18 (13× `ai-color-palette`, `text-overflow`, `single-font`, +2), table 14
(10× `ai-color-palette`, `nested-cards`, +2). Two of those browser findings are **false positives at the
reported location** and two are **true but mis-located**:
- `bounce-easing` and `layout-transition: height` are both attributed to `body`, whose computed
  `animation-name: none`, `transition-duration: 0s`, `transition-property: all`. The detector is reading the
  `--ease-pop` token's *declaration site* and the preflight `transition: all`. Nothing on `body` moves.
- `single-font` ("only font used is vazirmatn") is false as stated and worse in truth: `document.fonts.size`
  is **0**. No `@font-face` exists anywhere in the repo, no webfont file is shipped, and the page makes zero
  font requests. Neither Archivo nor Vazirmatn ever loads.
- `ai-color-palette` ("Cyan neon text on dark background", 32 element hits) fires on the brand accent itself:
  `--volt: #1fbfa0` is hue 168°, inside the detector's 160–200° band, on `--bg-base: #081613`.
- `text-overflow` is real: measured on `/profile` at 390px, two destructive/settings subtitles are clipped.

**Visual overlays** are visible only in the headless run used for this review; the live server was stopped and
`index.html` was left byte-identical (`git diff index.html` empty).

## Overall Impression

A specific, well-built football product with a broken typography layer and two real correctness bugs in the
newest feature. The shell, the token discipline, the state machine and the RTL work are better than most
shipped sports apps. What is off is not taste — it is that the app promises a type system it never loads, a
calendar week that starts on the wrong day for its primary audience, and a match screen that shows two
anonymous crests.

## What's Working

1. **The colour contract is real and holds where it is inconvenient.** Volt = action, gold = "your club",
   danger = destructive, ink-2/ink-3 = depth. Verified computed values: sheet `rgb(18,44,37)` (elevated),
   day numbers `rgb(237,247,243)` (text-ink), volt fill `rgb(31,191,160)`. The gold star means "your anchor
   club" in the feed *and* "crowd rating" in the ratings — one hue, two jobs, but consistently applied.
2. **Three distinct load states and honest copy.** `Skeleton` / `EmptyState` / `ErrorState` are separate
   components; "No key events yet." ships with "Goals, cards and subs appear live — check back at kickoff",
   and the feed footer always states "Live scores refresh every 30–60s · Data as of 02:47 PM". The app never
   pretends to be fresher than it is.
3. **The date strip does the hard part right.** Today auto-centres on mount (0.0px offset), the selected chip
   smooth-centres, past days sit left in LTR and right in RTL, and the row is a `role="group"` of
   `aria-pressed` toggles rather than a fake tablist that promises arrow-key semantics it does not implement.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] No webfont ever loads — the type system is a spec for a typeface that does not ship

`tokens.css:33-34` declares `--font-display: "Archivo", "Vazirmatn", sans-serif` and
`--font-body: "Vazirmatn", "Archivo", sans-serif`. The repo has **no `@font-face` rule** (grep over `src/`,
`index.html`, `tokens.css` → 0), **no font file** (0 `.woff2` in the tree) and **no font `<link>`** in
`index.html`. Measured in Chrome: `document.fonts.size === 0`, zero font network requests, and a canvas
measurement of a Persian string in `"Vazirmatn"` is 76.05px against 76.49px for plain `sans-serif` — i.e. the
family is not resolving.

**Why it matters:** every rule the CSS reasons about — `html[lang="fa"] .label { font-size: 12px }` with the
comment "Vazirmatn at 11px is also below the Persian reading floor" — is reasoning about a font that never
arrives. On the target hardware (low-end Android webviews, iOS Telegram) neither family is installed, so the
app renders in the system sans and the Persian in the OS naskh fallback: the modern, geometric Persian face
the whole brand was designed around is absent. The recent 4-step type scale, the tabular numerals and the
Persian tracking rules are all being applied to a substitute.

**Fix:** self-host the two families. Add `@font-face` blocks for Vazirmatn (400/500/700/800) and Archivo
(600/700/800) pointing at subset `.woff2` files in `public/fonts/`, `font-display: swap`, and a
`preload` for the body face. Keep the Persian file subset to Arabic ranges and the Latin to `latin`. If the
display face is not wanted for Persian, scope `--font-display` to `html[lang="en"]`.

### [P1] The calendar button is 227px off-screen, and the strip gives no scroll affordance

Measured on first paint at 390×720: the button sits at `x = 617` in LTR and `x = -271` in RTL — outside the
viewport in both directions, at the far end of a 21-chip strip whose `scrollWidth` is 1272 against a
`clientWidth` of 358. The strip clips its overflow with no edge fade, no chevron, no "more" hint.

**Why it matters:** the calendar is the *only* route to any date beyond three weeks, and it is invisible until
the user happens to scroll the strip all the way to its end. The strip's own comment says it exists "for the
days the strip cannot reach" — that promise is invisible to the user it was made for.

**Fix:** pin the calendar button outside the scroll container (a flex row: scroller + fixed trailing button),
or overlay it on the strip's trailing edge with a gradient mask so the clipped chip reads as "more". Either
way the affordance must be on screen at 390px, in both directions.

### [P1] The Persian calendar week starts on Sunday, not Saturday

Measured header order, visual left→right: `شن جمع پن چه سه دو یک` — so the **rightmost** column (the first
column in an RTL reading order) is "یک" (Sunday), and Saturday ("شن") sits in the leftmost column. Iranian
weeks begin on Saturday. The column→weekday *mapping* is correct (1 October lands in the "پن" column, as it
should), but the week is rotated one day from the audience's convention.

**Why it matters:** a Persian user scanning the grid for "Saturday" reads the leftmost column, which is where
a Sunday-first grid puts the end of the week. Every date they pick is off by one in their head.

**Fix:** reorder `DOW_FA` to `["شن","یک","دو","سه","چه","پن","جمع"]` and shift the leading-blank count to
`(first.getDay() + 1) % 7` so the grid still puts each date in its true weekday column. The FA strip chips
use single letters and are unaffected; the English grid keeps Sunday-first.

### [P1] The match screen never names the teams before kickoff

`MatchDetail.tsx:232-300` renders the score hero as: a league+kickoff line, a `TeamBadge` crest per side,
the kickoff clock, and a countdown chip — with the team **names** deliberately omitted ("Badge only: the user
tapped this match's card, so the crest is all the teams need — the scoreboard below already says who plays").
Before kickoff there is no scoreboard: Timeline shows "No key events yet.", Stats and Lineups show their own
empty states, and the header says "Match" over "La Liga".

**Why it matters:** the first screen of a football app shows two anonymous crests and a time. A user who
arrived from a bot link, a push notification or a shared URL cannot tell who is playing. The names only
appear once a goal lands.

**Fix:** put the two team names in the hero, under their crests (`text-sm font-bold text-ink`, truncated),
and keep the crest as the supporting element. The hero then reads *who* is playing, *when*, and *how long* —
in that order.

### [P2] The match hero prints the same two facts three times

Above the clock: `La Liga · Wed 09:30 PM`. Below it, larger: `09:30 PM`. And "La Liga" appears a third time
as the TopBar subtitle. The kickoff time — the one number that matters on a pre-match screen — is set in
muted `text-ink-2` while the countdown chip beside it is the only volt element.

**Fix:** one kickoff line (`Wed 09:30 PM` in `text-ink`, with the league only in the header), and let the
countdown chip carry the volt accent. Drop the league from the hero entirely — the header already says it.

### [P2] The "pick your clubs" banner's controls are flush with its border

Measured insets inside the banner card: the "Choose" pill (44px tall) sits **1px** from the top and bottom
border, and the 44px dismiss "X" sits **1px** from the top/bottom and **5px** from the trailing edge. The
body text has a healthy 7px inset.

**Why it matters:** a pill whose rounded edge kisses the card's rounded edge reads as a mis-registered
element rather than a deliberate one, and a 44px target 5px from the border is an easy mis-tap in a one-handed
grip.

**Fix:** give the banner `py-1.5` and `pe-1.5`, and let the CTA and the X keep their 44px hit areas inside
that padding.

### [P2] The list entrance stagger is still inert for the feed, the table and the leaderboard

`--stagger` is set in five files — `MatchCard.tsx`, `RatingRow.tsx`, `Table.tsx`, `Leaderboards.tsx`,
`Login.tsx` — but only `.timeline-in` (`index.css:410`) reads it:
`animation-delay: var(--stagger, 0ms)`. `.list-in` (`index.css:670`) and `.rise-in` (`index.css:324`) declare
an animation with no delay, so every one of those setters is dead code.

**Fix:** add `animation-delay: var(--stagger, 0ms);` to `.list-in` and `.rise-in`, and cap the delay
(`Math.min(index, 6) * 30ms`) so the last row is not held at `opacity: 0` under `both` fill. The
reduced-motion block already zeroes the delay.

### [P2] Two Profile subtitles are clipped at 390px, one of them a destructive-action warning

Measured on `/profile` at 390×720: "Clears pins, comments and saved preferences on this device" overflows its
box by 22px, and "Removes your account and everything in it — permanently" by 10px. Both are `truncate`d, so
the text ends in an ellipsis.

**Why it matters:** "Removes your account and everything in it — perma…" is the warning that is supposed to
make a user hesitate before deleting. It is cut off at exactly the point where the warning lands.

**Fix:** let these two rows wrap to two lines (`whitespace-normal`, `leading-snug`) instead of truncating —
they are the only long strings on the screen, and the row has the vertical room.

### [P2] The pre-match countdown chip pulses forever

`MatchDetail.tsx:288` — `<Chip tone="volt" className="motion-safe:animate-pulse">` on the "STARTS IN 6H 30M"
chip. The chip animates for the six hours before kickoff, continuously.

**Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md reserves motion for goal moments and live state ("Earned spectacle"); a
perpetually pulsing element on a resting screen is the one place the app sprays energy it has not earned. It
also makes the screen feel like something is happening when nothing is.

**Fix:** drop `animate-pulse`, or pulse only inside the final 60 minutes (`countdownLabel` already knows the
remaining time).

### [P3] The toast wears the one border the design system bans

`Toast.tsx:23,29,34` — `border-s-4` on all four tones: a 4px accent bar on the leading edge of every toast.
`reference/colorize.md:84` is explicit: hairline borders, "**not side-stripes; see the absolute ban on
`border-left/right > 1px`**". The detector flags it as `side-tab`, the shape it calls the most recognizable
tell of AI-generated UI.

**Fix:** drop `border-s-4` and keep the 1px tone border plus the existing 12–14% tone wash — the tint already
carries the tone; the bar is redundant with it.

### [P3] The brand accent sits inside the detector's "AI cyan" band

`--volt: #1fbfa0` is hue 168° on `--bg-base: #081613` (luminance 0.006). The detector's neon-text rule fires
on hue 160–200° with chroma ≥ 80 — which is 32 element hits across four screens, i.e. the app's own accent
colour, on purpose.

**Why it matters:** "cyan on dark" is the most recognisable AI-era palette, and the app's accent is 8° inside
the band. Rotating the accent 10–15° toward green (hue ~150–155) keeps "broadcast teal" but separates it
from the default AI cyan.

**Fix:** trial a 12° hue rotation of `--volt` (and its `--text-on-volt` pairing) and compare the four screens
side by side before committing.

### [P3] Persian copy names the same screen two different things

`matches.title` is "Matches" in English and "مچ‌دی" in Persian, while the tab label is "مسابقات" — the same
destination with two names in the same language. `dateStrip.label` is "Select matchday" in English and
"روز فعلی را انتخاب کنید" ("select the current day") in Persian, which describes one chip rather than the
row.

**Fix:** make the FA page title "مسابقات" (or the tab "مچ‌دی"), and align the FA strip label with the English
one ("روز مسابقه را انتخاب کنید").

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** no keyboard navigation across the date strip — the component explicitly declines
`role="tab"` semantics, so days are reachable only by Tab-and-Enter, 21 times. No jump-to-today, no search,
and the route to a date outside three weeks starts 227px off-screen. The feed has no filtering beyond the
league chips.

**Jordan (first-timer):** the match screen shows two crests and no names. The first-run feed is one banner
row ("Pick your clubs to see their matches first") with a "Choose" button and an X that dismisses it — one
tap and the only guidance is gone. The 404 screen is genuinely good, and is the one screen that tells the
user what to do next.

**Casey (one-handed, on the move):** the calendar button lives at the far end of a horizontal scroll — a
two-finger gesture to reach a control that is 227px past the right edge. The refresh action is a small icon in
the top-right corner, the least reachable real estate on the device, for the action a live-score user wants
most. The tab bar is good.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard / low vision):** the ARIA work is strong (skip link, trapped Tab in sheets,
focus restore, `aria-pressed` on chips, `aria-current="date"` on today, live regions on toasts). What fails
is content: the Persian calendar week starts on Sunday, the FA strip label says "select the current day", the
destructive-action warnings are visually clipped, and the only fixture indicator is a 2px dot in `text-ink-3`
— invisible to low vision, and the whole "scannable without tapping" promise rests on it.

**Riley (stress tester):** picking a date outside the synced window lands on an honest empty feed rather than
a broken one, and the sheet's dot marker honestly shows which days have data. The one soft spot is the
calendar's month navigation having no bound — it will happily page into a year with no fixtures at all.

## Minor Observations

- The calendar grid draws a 1px border on every one of its 31–35 day cells — more ink than the strip's chips,
  for the same information the strip already shows.
- The 2px fixture dots under the strip chips (`h-0.5 w-0.5 bg-ink-3`) are below the threshold where they
  read at a glance; the commit that added them promised "scannable without tapping".
- The Table page shows a full-width "Table" button directly under the league heading, above the table it
  names.
- The Profile header carries an unexplained "ACCOUNT" pill next to the edit pencil.
- `--ease-pop` is declared in both `tokens.css` and the `@theme` block in `index.css` — harmless, but it is
  the kind of duplication that drifts.
- The match hero's countdown chip is the only all-caps string in the app; every other label is sentence case.

## Questions to Consider

- If the accent moved 12° toward green, would the app still read as "broadcast teal" — or is the cyan-ness
  the identity?
- What if the match screen's headline were the two team names, with the time demoted to a caption?
- Does the feed need a strip that scrolls, or a strip that snaps?
- If the type system were actually loading tomorrow, which of the recent spacing/type commits would still
  hold — and which were tuned against a font nobody can see?
